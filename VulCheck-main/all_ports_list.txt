3. DYNAMIC / EPHEMERAL PORTS (49152 - 65535)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Range: 49152 to 65535
Description: Private, Dynamic, or Ephemeral Ports.
These ports cannot be officially registered or assigned to services.
Operating systems allocate ports from this range automatically to
serve as temporary source ports for outbound communication.
